CLARITY & ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

Direct, factual answers to common questions about engaging with AI Automation Labs.

Do we need to replace our existing systems?

+

Not necessarily. In most cases, we work with your existing ERP (such as SAP, Oracle, or Tally) and legacy applications. We assess the environment and determine whether lightweight API middleware, targeted modernization, or replacement is commercially sensible.

Do we need to know exactly what to build before reaching out?

+

No. Most clients come to us knowing where operational friction hurts—delayed reports, manual errors, or scaling limits. The Transformation Audit exists specifically to diagnose root causes and establish a prioritized implementation roadmap.

Can you work with our proprietary or legacy internal software?

+

Yes. Where required database access, network interfaces, or programmatic hooks exist, we engineer custom connectors and data bridges. We verify technical feasibility during the initial discovery phase.

How long does a typical digital transformation take?

+

The timeline depends on scope. The diagnostic Transformation Audit takes 14 days. High-impact implementation pilots typically go live in 6 to 10 weeks, delivering immediate operational value while broader systems are connected in phased sprints.

How do you measure success and return on investment?

+

We define clear, quantifiable operational benchmarks before writing any code: manual hours eliminated, order-to-dispatch latency reduction, reporting cycle time, and data reconciliation error rates.

What happens after implementation?

+

We ensure comprehensive knowledge transfer to your team. Depending on your preference, we can transition full stewardship internally or provide ongoing managed engineering, SLA monitoring, and continuous system enhancement.

Do we have to commit to a large company-wide programme immediately?

+

No. A defined audit or focused implementation for a single high-friction department is the recommended starting point. Further initiatives are prioritized based on demonstrable business value.
