Why Established Businesses Stall

Growing from ₹30Cr to ₹300Cr usually forces companies into a trap: critical knowledge gets trapped inside isolated spreadsheets, WhatsApp groups, and individual managers' memories. Core ERPs like SAP or Tally remain siloed from the actual physical shop floor.
